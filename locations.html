Direct Air Capture and Storage in Stockton, CA

Socio-Environmental Context

Stockton, California, is a vibrant city located in the heart of the Central Valley. Its unique environment presents both opportunities and challenges for innovative projects like Direct Air Capture (DAC):

  • Water Scarcity: Stockton frequently experiences droughts and relies on limited water resources. Responsible water use and conservation are crucial.
  • Air Quality: The region faces ongoing air quality challenges. Projects that minimize emissions and prioritize clean energy can make a positive impact.
  • Land Use: With a mix of urban, industrial, and agricultural areas, thoughtful site selection helps protect natural habitats and farmland.
Socioeconomic Landscape

Stockton is home to a diverse and resilient population. Understanding local conditions is key to creating lasting benefits:

  • Diversity: The city is made up of many cultures and backgrounds, contributing to its rich community life.
  • Economic Opportunity: While Stockton has affordable housing and a strong logistics sector, it also faces higher unemployment and lower median incomes compared to the rest of California.
  • Public Health: Local health outcomes are improving, but challenges like asthma and chronic disease remain. Clean air and job creation can help address these issues.
Why Community Engagement is Essential

Collaboration with Stockton’s communities isn’t just good practice—it’s essential for project success.

  • Local Insights: Residents and local leaders have valuable knowledge about environmental and economic priorities.
  • Shared Benefits: By working together, DAC projects can create jobs, support local businesses, and improve quality of life.
  • Trust and Transparency: Open communication builds trust and ensures that community concerns are addressed early and effectively.
