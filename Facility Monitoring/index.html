Before and After Facility Construction

We want to transparently communicate the impacts of building our facility and ensure that building this facility does not worsen the air quality in San Joaguin Valley.

Through continuing engagement sessions and through partnering with community leaders, we built consensus on where to place air monitors and how to establish a baseline for pre-construction pollution levels. We have established a network of air quality monitors both at the facility and at key locations in the community. We have also taken baseline monthly measurements in all these locations a year before facility construction began. This ensures that we can detect any shift in air quality that could be caused by building the facility.

Facility Monitoring

Removing CO2 directly from the atmosphere can use lots of water and energy, so we will be transparent aabout how much resources our facility uses each month.

We have optimized our process to maximize the use of recycled water, and we have prioritized using solar and wind to power the facility. Since there will be times when solar and wind energy are not available, we have also outfitted a nearby gas power plant with carbon capture and storage, ensuring all the energy to power our facility is decarbonized.
