Air Quality

We want to ensure that pollution caused from connstruction and operation of our facilty does not put undue risk on the community. To start, we prioritize decarbonized construction processes to construct our facility. In addition, we are setting up a network of air monitors around the facility. You can explore the current monitors on the interactive map, and we welcome further discussion on air quality and sensor placement from anyone in the community.

Resource Usage

It's difficult to remove carbon dioxide from the air, Direct Air capture can use lots of energy and water. The process needs energy to power lerge industrial fans, and needs water to regenerate solvents. These energy inputs eneable continuous operation of the facility.
