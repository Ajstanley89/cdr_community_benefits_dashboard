CO2 Ass-kickers Inc, We remove carbon dioxide directly from the air to fight climate change. Using advanced technology, we’re working to clean up the atmosphere and build a better future.

Site Location Map

This map shows where CO₂ Ass-Kickers Inc.’s Direct Air Capture site will be located, the well injection location (where the CO2 is stored), and the additional energy sources created to support the facility.

Direct Air Capture needs to be powered by low-carbon energy sources for effective carbon capture. Where possible, we will prioritize the usage of wind and solar energy to power the facilty.

If there is not sufficient solar or wind energy, we plan to use a gas power plant with carbon capture and storage fittings to power the facility.

Explore the map to learn more about the location and energy sources for our proposed facility.

Environmental Impacts

Our project aims to protect the environment while capturing and storing CO2. We only use about 792,000 gallons of water a day and regularly track PM2.5 emissions through Purple Air Monitors. Click below to learn more about this.

Job Impacts

WThe construction of this facility will create 765 jobs. When the facility is operational, it will create 220 long-term jobs for Stockton. We believe that upfront investment in quality labor will benefit both our project and the community, so we prioritize hiring union jobs.

Environmental Risk

Our Plan to monitor air quality

We're ensuring that construction of this facility:
  • Doesn't Cause Additional Pollution Burden to the Community
  • Doesn't create significant CO2 Emissions
To do this, we're commited to a fully decarbonized trucking fleet to ship all construction materials. We've also established a network of air quality sensors to monitor any changes in air quality around the facility.
