Site Location Map

This map shows where TBD Company Name's Direct Air Capture site will be located, the well injection location (where the CO2 is stored), and the additional energy sources created to support the facility.

Direct Air Capture uses a lot of energy. Removing a tonne of CO2 from the atmosphere requires about 1500 kWh. This is just above the monthly energy consumption of a family of 4 in a 2000 sqft home. For reliable CO2 removal, DACS needs to be powered by low-carbon energy sources.

We have opted into Ava Community Energy’s Renewable 100 program, which sources 100% of its electricity from California wind and solar. By aligning our power needs with fully renewable sources, we ensure that our Direct Air Capture systems are driven by clean energy—maximizing climate benefit and minimizing upstream emissions

While we prioritize using low carbon energy sources like wind and solar to power our facility, these resources alone aren't enough to meet our energy needs. Because of this, we have outfitted a nearby gas power plant with carbon capture and storage technology to ensure that this facility is fully powered by decarbonized sources.

Community Engagement Philosophy

As we seek to restore the environment, we also seek to build community. Scaling up a technology this new requires community trust, and this project would not have been possible without the direct partnership of the people of Stockton

Every piece of this project was made through building consensus with the community. Our process involved:

  • Holding Recurring Town Hall meetings early and often
  • Meeting directly with community leaders to build consensus on what to monitor
  • Commitment to first consider candidates from Stockton when hiring

We chose the San Joaquin Valley first and foremost for its people and exemplary workfornce. It is a priveledge to serve your community.

Environmental Impacts

Our project aims to protect the environment while capturing and storing CO2. We only use about 792,000 gallons of water a day and regularly track PM2.5 emissions through Purple Air Monitors. Click below to learn more about this.

Job Impacts

The construction of this facility will create 765 jobs. When the facility is operational, it will create 220 long-term jobs for Stockton. We believe that upfront investment in quality labor will benefit both our project and the community, so we prioritize hiring union jobs.
