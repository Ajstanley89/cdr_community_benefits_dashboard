Site Location Map

This map shows where TBD Company Name's Direct Air Capture site will be located, the well injection location (where the CO2 is stored), and the additional energy sources created to support the facility.

Direct Air Capture uses a lot of energy. Removing a tonne of CO2 from the atmosphere requires about 1500 kWh. This is just above the monthly energy consumption of a family of 4 in a 2000 sqft home. For reliable CO2 removal, DACS needs to be powered by low-carbon energy sources.

We have opted into Ava Community Energy’s Renewable 100 program, which sources 100% of its electricity from California wind and solar. By aligning our power needs with fully renewable sources, we ensure that our Direct Air Capture systems are driven by clean energy—maximizing climate benefit and minimizing upstream emissions

If there is not sufficient solar or wind energy, we have outfitted a nearby gas power plant with carbon capture and storage to power our facility.

Job Impacts

The construction of this facility will create 765 jobs. When the facility is operational, it will create 220 long-term jobs for Stockton. We believe that upfront investment in quality labor will benefit both our project and the community, so we prioritize hiring union jobs.
